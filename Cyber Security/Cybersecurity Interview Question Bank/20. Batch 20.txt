# Batch 20 — Blockchain Security

- Primary syllabus: Section 36 — Optional Blockchain Security

The progression moves from blockchain architecture and Solidity fundamentals to EVM execution, smart contract vulnerabilities, DeFi attack chains, auditing, remediation and security architecture.

All offensive exercises assume owned contracts, local testnets, explicitly authorized environments or isolated simulations.
